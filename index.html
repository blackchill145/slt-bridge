info About SLT Bridge

SLT Bridge is a graduation project proposal under the College of Science, Department of Computer Science, Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST).

The goal is to provide a zero-dependency, local web-based communication tool that leverages browser machine learning and text-to-speech to make sign language translation cheap, accessible, and fast. Built as a web application, it works directly in browser sandboxes, avoiding complicated app installations.

Version 1.0.0 (Web) KNUST Project GSL-Aligned SignTalk-GH Integrated
